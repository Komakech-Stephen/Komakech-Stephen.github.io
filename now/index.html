What I'm doing

Quality Assurance leadership at Agri Exim Ltd — overseeing quality control, HACCP monitoring, food safety compliance, and team supervision. In parallel, I'm pursuing my BSc in Networking and Cybersecurity at ISBAT University and actively building cybersecurity skills through hands-on projects and certifications.

Three things are getting most of my evening / weekend cycles right now:

  • Cybersecurity Projects — Building hands-on experience with SIEM monitoring (Elastic Stack, Kibana), firewall/IDS deployment (Sophos, pfSense, Snort, Suricata), penetration testing tools (Nmap, Nessus, OpenVAS), and network security simulations using Cisco Packet Tracer.

What I'm learning

Two threads, deliberately kept in parallel because I think they sharpen each other:

  • Cybersecurity technical depth. Network security, SIEM monitoring, incident response, penetration testing, and security operations. Building hands-on experience through home lab projects and certification preparation (CCNA, Check Point CCSA/CCSE in progress).
  • Quality Assurance leadership. HACCP monitoring, food safety compliance, team supervision, quality control processes, and operational excellence. Six years of experience in quality assurance with proven leadership in food safety and quality management.

The bet is that fluency in both sides — operational rigor from quality Assurance and technical depth in cybersecurity — is a more durable skill than depth in either one alone. Quality assurance teaches attention to detail, process thinking, and compliance mindset — all crucial for effective security operations.
